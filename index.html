A Little About Me

I'm in my final year of Frontend Development at Nackademin, graduating in May 2027. I enjoy taking an idea from an early sketch to a working application, thinking through both the code and the experience of using it.

My main focus is React and JavaScript. Through my projects, I have also worked with Angular, TypeScript, C#/.NET, APIs and databases, developing a broader understanding of how an application fits together.

Before studying development, I worked in IT support and ran my own shop. Those experiences taught me to listen, take responsibility and solve practical problems. Outside development, I coach youth football — another place where communication, teamwork and helping others improve matter to me.
